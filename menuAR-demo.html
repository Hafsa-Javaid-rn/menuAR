The process

Live in just 2-3 Days

No developers needed. No app to install. We handle the hard part.

1
Sign up & upload
Create your restaurant profile and upload your menu items with photos and pricing.
2
We build your 3D models
Our team converts your food photos into detailed, accurate 3D models using AI + manual refinement.
3
Get your QR code
Print and place on your tables. Customers scan to open your live AR menu — no app download needed.
4
Track & grow

What you get

Everything your restaurant needs

A complete AR menu platform — not just a 3D viewer.

Real-time 3D viewer
Customers rotate, zoom, and inspect every detail of a dish before ordering. Works in any browser, no app needed.
Augmented reality
On any iPhone or Android, customers tap "View in AR" and the food appears on their actual table. No app download.
Analytics dashboard
See which items get the most views, average session duration, peak hours, and how AR engagement correlates with orders.
QR code menu
A unique QR code for your restaurant. Customers scan it at the table and see your full 3D menu instantly.
Self-serve dashboard
Add new items, update prices, mark items as unavailable, and manage your entire menu — all without contacting us.
Multi-branch support
Running multiple locations? Manage all branches from one account, with location-specific menus and pricing.
